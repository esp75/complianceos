# Coverage

Which jurisdictions and instruments the knowledge pack models today. Every record is `draft` until a
licensing practitioner reviews it. [`knowledge/VERIFICATION.md`](../../knowledge/VERIFICATION.md)
says how each part was checked and what still needs a human.

| Jurisdiction | What is modelled |
|---|---|
| ITU | Advance publication, coordination request, notification and bringing into use, the 7-year limit, Resolution 35 milestones for constellations |
| France | Space operations authorisation (LOS), ANFR satellite frequency filing, ministerial authorisation to operate frequency assignments |
| Austria | Space activity approval (Weltraumgesetz 2011, Weltraumverordnung 2015). Ground-station radio licence (TKG 2021 §§ 28, 34, Fernmeldebüro). Austria names no ITU filing administration; the operator's ITU coordination documents are part of the space approval (WRV § 2(6)) |
| Germany | Frequency assignment for a ground station and the ITU satellite filing, both with the Bundesnetzagentur (TKG §§ 91, 92, 95). No space-activity authorisation, registration or general assignments researched |
| Netherlands | Frequency licence for a ground station (Telecommunicatiewet art. 3.13(1)), issued through the RDI. The ITU satellite filing route and space-activity rules not researched |
| Ireland | Wireless telegraphy licence for a ground station (Wireless Telegraphy Act 1926, revised), granted by ComReg. The ITU satellite filing route not researched |
| Canada | Radio authorisation for a ground station (Radiocommunication Act ss. 4(1), 5(1)), issued under the Minister of Industry (ISED). ITU filing route and exemptions not researched |
| Norway | Frequency permit for a ground station (ekomloven § 11-2) and satellite system filing to the ITU register on application (§ 11-16), both delegated to Nkom. Fees and conditions not researched |
| Denmark | Radio frequency permit for a ground station (Lov om radiofrekvenser § 6(1)), issued by Digitaliseringsstyrelsen. ITU filing route and satellite band rules not researched |
| Spain | Title for using the radio public domain for a ground station (Ley 11/2022 art. 88(3)), with its six-week rule and coordination exception. Orbit-spectrum reservation to the State for communications satellites (art. 85(5)). Ground-station form, fees and royal decree not researched |
| Luxembourg | Space activity authorisation (2020 law), satellite system concession |
| United Kingdom | Orbital operator licence (CAA, SIA 2018 / OSA 1986), Ofcom satellite filing, earth station licence |
| United States | FCC Part 25 space station licence and streamlined small-satellite route, US market access, earth station licence, NOAA remote sensing licence |

Countries in a mission that have no data are named in the roadmap's warnings rather than skipped.
To add one, see [how to add or correct a jurisdiction](../how-to/add-or-correct-a-jurisdiction.md).
